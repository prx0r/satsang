Sit and let attention rest on the whole body at once: not one part, the whole field of sensation. Then take the body, and the world around it, as made of awareness. Let this be felt rather than thought. When the mind narrows back to "me" and "mine", return to the wider field and rest there. The body stays where it is; only the sense of it being separate softens.

If you feel disoriented, unreal or anxious, open the eyes, feel the feet and the seat, and stop for today.

If you have a history of trauma, dissociation, psychosis or severe anxiety, keep sits short with eyes open, or practise with a qualified teacher or clinician.

The rest of the Vijñāna Bhairava (breath retention, mantra, the subtle-body and burning practices) is learned from a qualified teacher in the Trika tradition; Satsang lists places to practise.
