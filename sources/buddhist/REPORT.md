## Coverage notes and gaps (honest)
- **Rebirth is thin (7 clips).** Official 1–20 min uploads on rebirth are rare. Most rebirth talks (Ajahn Brahm, Plum Village) run 60+ min. I cut Brahm's "The Process of Rebirth" and "What Happens After You Die?" into virtual clips, but the transcript windows mostly score as death or kamma, because the auto-captions say "rebirth" rarely. Death (87), awakening (76), suffering (52), kamma (31) and compassion (47) are well covered. Self/not-self (17) and emptiness (3) are moderate to thin. The next pass should add more virtual clips from rebirth and anatta talks (see the backlog below).
- **Zen is thinner than the other traditions as natural clips.** San Francisco Zen Center's Shunryu Suzuki archive talks mostly run 20–40 min. Their auto-captions are almost empty (old audio: about 1,100 words caught in 23 min), so neither topic tagging nor virtual cuts worked for "Not Sticking to Enlightenment" (i2vBYrco5gU) or "Genjo Koan" (3qkfhVv6RZc). Zen is covered by Okumura (Sanshin; 6 virtual clips including Dōgen on death), Shukman (Mu koan virtual clips), Roshi Joan Halifax (Upaya), Brad Warner, Shinge Roshi (Zen Studies Society; mostly sesshin opening and closing remarks) and Thich Nhat Hanh (Plum Village).
- **Pa-Auk / Burmese: no clips.** The only official channel found is Pa-Auk Meditation Centre Singapore. It has very few English talks under 20 min. The one long English Q&A I transcribed (K14RZURM458) produced no usable windows because the captions are poor on heavily accented speech. Pa-Auk Sayadaw, Mahasi Sayadaw and Goenka are added as **figures** only. Sayadaw U Tejaniya's YouTube channel was not confirmed as official (his teachings site credits Ven. Khema Sara as the recorder), so I skipped it.
- **Rob Burbea: skipped.** hermesamara.org (Hermes Amāra Foundation, the official custodian) hosts his talks as its own audio plus a Vimeo link. It points to no YouTube channel. ## Virtual clips: method
- Timed transcripts come from the `analyze_video` tool. They are stored INTERNAL ONLY at `work/wiser/transcripts-internal/buddhist/<id>.*.srt` (29 talks). No transcript text appears in any output. The existing `transcripts-internal/*.txt` HH files are summarised transcriptions with **no timestamps**, so they could not place start/end. Every HH virtual clip uses a new timed SRT.
- Windows are 5–14 min (300–840 s; a few run to 810 s). The talk is split into 20 s blocks and scored by topic-keyword density from `topics.json` (plus the proposed leaves) with core-topic weights. A topic must be spread across the window (≥25% of blocks), not one burst. Windows don't overlap, there are at most 4 per talk, and both ends snap to a caption pause. Topics on these rows carry `src: transcript` (0.75 lead, 0.6 secondary).
- Row fields: `sec` = passage length (end − start); `dur` = full parent video length; `band` from `sec`; `virtual: true`. HH virtual rows copy the parent's HH metadata (code, published, views) and drop the parent's `full`, `passage` and `tier`.
- `segTitle` / `title` = "<Topic label>: <parent title>". This is mechanical and worth an editorial pass before publishing.
- `analyze_video` rate-limited heavily (HTTP 429 when calls run in parallel), so I made calls one at a time. I didn't log any step as a failure: each 429 cleared on a single retry.
