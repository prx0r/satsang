## Scholarly and spiritual lineages (lineages.json)
Every edge carries a URL I opened or searched on 2026-10-20, plus a confidence level. Key chains:
- **Altmann school (Brandeis):** Alexander Altmann supervised Arthur Green, Elliot Wolfson and Daniel Matt. Moshe Idel took his PhD under Shlomo Pines and Ephraim Gottlieb (Hebrew University, 1976) and later succeeded to Scholem's chair.
- **Tehran / Traditionalist:** Nasr supervised Chittick (PhD 1974). Schuon taught Lings and Nasr; Guénon influenced Lings.
- **Corbin → Hillman → Cheetham:** Hillman called Jung and Corbin the "first" and "second fathers" of archetypal psychology; Cheetham's books study Corbin and Hillman. Vervaeke's Meaning Crisis eps. 48–49 build on Corbin.
- **Hadot:** influenced Foucault (Davidson 1990). The Hadot → Vervaeke edge is marked low confidence (podcast summary only).
- **Paris–Amsterdam–Exeter:** Faivre (first chair, EPHE 1979) influenced Hanegraaff. Hanegraaff founded the Amsterdam HHP chair (1999); Egil Asprem did his PhD there (2013, medium confidence; the formal supervisor is not named) after an MA under Marco Pasi. Goodrick-Clarke founded EXESESO (2005; closed 2014).
- **Voss circle (Canterbury):** Voss and Cornelius created the Kent (2006–11) and Canterbury Christ Church (2014–21) MAs. Attwood, Livingstone and Voss co-founded the Centre (2019). Simon Wilson and Linden West supervised Livingstone's PhD.
- **Prometheus Trust:** Tim Addey founded it in 1986. It republished Thomas Taylor (33 vols), whose translations of Iamblichus and Proclus are in texts.json. John Dillon is a patron and Crystal Addey sits on its editorial board; both are recorded in a note only, not as edges.
- **Orders:** Daghestani → Nazim → Hisham Kabbani → Nour Kabbani. Inayat Khan → Vilayat → Pir Zia. Munes Ali Shah → Javad → Alireza Nurbakhsh. Main → Freeman (WCCM). Keating → Bourgeault, and Gurdjieff influenced her. Merton → Finley. Rohr founded CAC.

## Gaps and caveats
- **No virtual clips.** yt-dlp could not fetch player responses or chapters ("Failed to extract any player response") for any of the 16 long talks I tried. I stopped after the second failure, per the spec. Long talks over 60 min were left out (for example Hanegraaff's Embassy lectures, Cheetham's Paradox of Monotheism, Voss's Daimon and Ficino talks, the Hanegraaff–Sledge roundtable, McGinn on Eckhart, Idel's long interview, Versluis on Plotinus). These are the first targets for a transcript-cut pass.
- **Thin scholarship in the main set.** Gregory Shaw, Crystal Addey, John Dillon, Algis Uždavinys, Sara Rappe, Pierre Hadot, Brian Copenhaver, Frances Yates, Antoine Faivre, Arthur Versluis (2), Nicholas Goodrick-Clarke, Henry Corbin, James Hillman, Jung, Patrick Harpur, Sachiko Murata, David Bentley Hart, Martin Laird and Bernard McGinn (1) have no official deep catalogue. Their talks are scattered across podcasts and university channels, often over 60 min or re-uploaded by fans, so I added none, or only a few in more voices. The Voss–Shaw–Addey teaching link is **not verified**: Addey did her PhD at Bristol (2009), and I found no source tying Shaw to the Canterbury MA.
- **Hanegraaff has 1 clip** within 60 min. His big lectures run 85–95 min.
- **Mevlevi, Chishti and Mount Athos:** no official channel with talks in English found. Mevlevi is covered indirectly through Rumi (Baraka Blue at the Rumi Center, Safi). Hesychasm rests on one teacher (Father Spyridon, 40 talks filtered to prayer, repentance and inner life; I left out his polemical titles on evolution, politics and the like).
- **Daniel Matt (5) and Wolfson (5)** fall short of 8 within 60 min. Their CSP and Wolfson lectures mostly run 58–70 min.
- **Partial listings:** YouTube rate-limited the continuation pages (shared IP with the other sourcers). Listings for WCCM, SufiLive, Lumen Christi and Seekers are partial. CAC came from `sources/western/raw/ch_cac.json` and ESOTERICA from `sources/deep-tantra/raw/flat/` (both read-only copies). Hebrew College, CSP and New Thinking Allowed came from in-channel search only.
- **Dates:** flat listings have no publish dates, so `published`/`year` are null.
- **Unverified ownership:** the "RecoveringtheSacred" channel (Nasr, Schuon, hesychasm lectures) was not used, because I could not confirm who owns it.
- Several titles are partly in Persian, flagged `lang: mixed`. Art Green's Hebrew-language versions were left out; the English versions are kept.
