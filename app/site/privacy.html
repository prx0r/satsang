Privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Satsang shows two dharma talks and asks which is wiser. You can use it without an account.

What we keep

  • Your picks without an account: stored in your browser, and sent to our database with a random device id (not linked to your name, email or IP address) so the crowd chart can learn. Requests are rate-limited using a one-way hash of your IP address that is discarded after an hour.
  • Watch signals: which talk you skipped or finished, and talks that failed to play, so broken ones can be removed.
  • With an account: your username, avatar choice, picks and profile settings. This device's earlier picks and saves join the account only if you say yes when you first sign in.
  • The shared account (auth.tantrafiles.xyz): one Google sign-in for Satsang, the journey journal and the practice lab. It keeps a random account id, a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your Google id, a pen name and when you signed up. No email, no name, no photo. Delete it any time from the account menu (Delete my account).
  • Your journal: what Satsang reads from your journal is worked out and kept on this device only. It is never sent to us.
  • Journeys (only if you join one): your entries are shared with that journey's members, encrypted at rest; members see a day's entries only after posting their own. Our server holds the keys, so this is not end-to-end encryption. Withdraw an entry or leave and everything you posted there is deleted.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking pixels, no selling data.
  • Talks play in YouTube's own player; YouTube's privacy policy applies to that player.

Where it lives

A Cloudflare D1 database operated for Satsang. To delete your data, clear it from Your model → History, or email hello@tantrafiles.xyz.
