Before Sao the person-chain ends — honestly. The inception bridge is textual,
not personal: the forest monks practice from the Pāli canon (early-suttas node
exists; no canon Text record yet — backlog), and the Atlas reaches further back:
Brihadaranyaka → influenced → the Buddha (the Hindu-stream root), Mahavira
parallels the Buddha (śramaṇa contemporary). So Amaravati traces:
Amaravati → Amaro → Chah → Mun → Sao → Pāli canon → the Buddha →
Brihadaranyaka (Upanishadic root).

Backlog: atlas person nodes for the Buddha's own teachers (Alara Kalama,
Uddaka Ramaputta); a canon Text record (Pāli Tipiṭaka) with editions
(SuttaCentral/ATI/BPS — see 09); the 7 orphan monks (Ahimsako, Chandako,
Cunda, Karuniko, Piak, Sona, Sudhiro) have no edges at all — likely Chah
students with unwritten edges, same class of work as Trika's orphans.
Note: Thanissaro surfaces as atlas reachback because his tradition tag sits
outside thai-forest — cross-tagging is correct here (he carries the Lee line
into early-suttas); the cluster builder counts him, the chain keeps him.
