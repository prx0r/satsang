# THE NIBBANA MOVIE — Level 1: Settle (shooting script)

Full script. Plain English only — no Pali spoken. TTS voice + visual +
practice, timed. ~25 minutes. This is the register for all 12 levels.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–2:00)

**VISUAL:** Black screen. A single point of warm light fades in at centre.
It breathes — gently brighter, gently dimmer. No text. No title. Just the
light, breathing, for thirty seconds.

**VOICE:**
> "Before anything can be seen, attention has to stay still long enough
> to look." [pause 10s]
> "That's the only problem we're solving today." [pause 10s]

**VISUAL:** The light steadies. Text fades in below it, small:
"Level 1 — Settle."

**VOICE:**
> "You don't need to believe anything. You don't need to know any words.
> You need a chair, ten minutes, and your breath." [pause 5s]

---

## PART 1: THE PROBLEM (2:00–7:00)

**VISUAL:** First-person. The screen is a room — blurred edges, shifting
focus. The view darts: a window, a phone, a thought about dinner, the
clock, back to the window. Each shift is quick, restless. Text overlay,
small: "this is your attention right now."

**VOICE:**
> "Watch what your attention just did. It went to the window. Then the
> phone. Then dinner. Then the clock." [pause 5s]
> "That's not a flaw. That's what attention does when nothing is holding
> it. It wanders, because wandering is its job." [pause 10s]
> "Meditation doesn't fix attention. It gives it somewhere to sit."
> [pause 10s]

**VISUAL:** The darting slows. The view settles on one thing — a hand
resting on a knee. The edges stop blurring. The frame goes still.

**VOICE:**
> "That hand. That's the whole practice. Rest attention there, and when
> it leaves, bring it back. That's it. That's Level 1." [pause 10s]

---

## PART 2: THE INSTRUCTION (7:00–10:00)

**VISUAL:** Simple diagram — a body outline, seated. A soft gold circle
marks the belly. The circle expands slightly on the in-breath, contracts
on the out-breath. Instruction text appears one line at a time.

**VOICE:**
> "Sit down. Any chair. Feet on the floor if you can." [pause 5s]
> "Feel the breath wherever it's clearest. Nose. Chest. Belly. Pick one."
> [pause 5s]
> "Now just stay there. When you notice you've left — and you will, in
> about ten seconds — come back. No scolding. Coming back IS the rep."
> [pause 10s]
> "That's the entire instruction. Everything else in this movie is built
> on those three sentences." [pause 5s]

**VISUAL:** The three sentences appear on screen, one at a time:
1. Feel the breath wherever it's clearest.
2. When you notice you've left, come back.
3. Coming back is the rep.

---

## PART 3: THE GUIDED SIT (10:00–20:00)

**VISUAL:** The breathing circle. Slow expansion and contraction. A timer
shows 10:00 counting down, small, in the corner. Nothing else.

**VOICE:**
> "Starting now. Ten minutes. I'll check in a few times. The rest is you
> and the breath." [pause 5s]
> "Feel the breath." [pause 60s]
> "If you've wandered, come back. That's a rep. Good." [pause 60s]
> "Still here? Good. If you left again, come back again. Every return
> counts." [pause 60s]
> "Halfway. Nothing has to happen. You're training the return, not
> achieving a state." [pause 60s]
> "Two minutes left. Let the breath be whatever it is. Ragged, smooth,
> shallow, deep — doesn't matter. Staying matters." [pause 90s]
> "Done. Notice how you feel. Not good or bad — just notice. That's data."
> [pause 10s]

---

## PART 4: THE GATE (20:00–24:00)

**VISUAL:** Back to the breathing circle, now still. Text appears:
"THE GATE."

**VOICE:**
> "Here's how you know Level 1 is done. Sit ten minutes without getting
> up. Not without thinking — without getting up." [pause 10s]
> "Do that three days in a row. The log proves it, not your opinion.
> Three ten-minute sits, three days. Then Level 2 unlocks." [pause 10s]
> "If you got up on day two — fine. Start the count again. The practice
> doesn't care. It just waits." [pause 10s]
> "What if you can't do ten minutes? Do five. Then six. Then seven.
> The gate is ten, but the path is yours. Build up to it." [pause 5s]

**VISUAL:** A simple log card: Day 1 [ ], Day 2 [ ], Day 3 [ ]. Checkboxes.
"Three checks. That's the whole gate."

---

## CLOSE (24:00–25:00)

**VISUAL:** The point of warm light from the cold open. Breathing gently.
Text, small: "Level 2 — The Theme. Unlocks after three sits."

**VOICE:**
> "You just did the only thing every level requires. You sat down, you
> stayed, you came back when you left." [pause 10s]
> "Everything from here is built on that. See you at Level 2." [pause 5s]

**VISUAL:** Fade to black. End card: the breathing light, the log card,
and one line: "Coming back is the rep."

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **TTS:** plain English throughout. No Pali spoken. No dharma terms.
  "Attention," "breath," "return," "rep" — gym language, not temple language.
- **Visuals:** breathing circle (deitybody grammar: midline + breath envelope).
  First-person room for the problem section. Diagram for instruction. Timer
  for the sit. Nothing else. Restraint IS the aesthetic.
- **Pauses:** timed as marked. The silence after each line is the teaching.
  Do not fill it with music. Room tone only.
- **Gate:** behavioural, logged, no self-report. Three 10-minute sits, three
  days. The runtime checks the log, not the user's opinion.
- **Trojan horse:** no mention of Buddhism, nibbāna, meditation tradition,
  or anything that requires belief. A chair, ten minutes, a breath. Anyone
  can do this. Everyone who does it is on the path whether they know it or not.
