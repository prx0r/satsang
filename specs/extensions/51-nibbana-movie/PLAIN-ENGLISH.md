# PLAIN-ENGLISH — the language law (2026-10-20, extension pack 51)

The viewer never has to learn a Pali word. Every concept arrives as plain
English describing something they already experience. Pali appears in
parentheses as citation — traceability to sources — never as required
vocabulary. Someone who completes all 12 levels without learning a single
Pali term has still lived the entire path.

## The rule in practice

- TTS scripts use the plain English column only. Pali never spoken.
- On-screen text may show Pali in small type under the English, as citation.
- Practice instructions never require knowing a term. "Notice the label being applied" — not "observe nimitta."
- Essays and study notes may use Pali freely (that's the study layer). The movie and the practices may not.
- FoJin verify_quote still runs on Pali citations in the study layer. The practice layer needs no Pali to function.
- If a sentence requires a Pali word to make sense, rewrite the sentence.
