# 24 — Abhinavagupta's corpus + the rights layer

**What:** the two missing crown texts (Parātrīśikāvivaraṇa, Īśvarapratyabhijñā-
vimarśinī/vivṛti) as records with author edges and works entries — plus
RIGHTS.md, the copyright regime the whole practices programme runs under,
and its gate.

**Files:** texts (2, linkless-by-rule — see RIGHTS), works-attach,
author edges, RIGHTS.md, rights-check-v1 (in 11), REPORT + MERGE-NOTES.

**Position (read RIGHTS.md first):** Sanskrit originals are public domain;
KSTS editions are public domain; modern translations are copyrighted and
get citation-only records (title/author/publisher/year — facts, not
expression) plus official publisher links at most, never scans. Practices
derived from copyrighted works are outline-only with teacher links;
initiated mantras are never reproduced; generated routines sequence and
time steps in our own words and link every step. Not legal advice —
edge cases go to the owner, and the report flow from 05 covers takedowns.
