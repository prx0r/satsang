# 10 — GOLDEN PATH: tradition → live teacher → method → guided practice → contact

**The endgame (user's words, 2026-10-20):** clean knowledge-graph data for
all traditions — what openpatala is for Sanskrit, for everything — eventually
housing all works, with educational things, videos and explainers on top.
The shape of it: *go straight to a tradition, find a live teacher, see their
exact method, be guided through it, then contact the teacher.*

The Padoux/matrika syllabus (sanskrithelp) and the Sivananda practice
(deitybody) are the reference implementations: structure-only syllabuses
that content renders from, and text→syllabus→visualisation pipelines with
human-gated promotion.
