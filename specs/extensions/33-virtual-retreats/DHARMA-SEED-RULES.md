# Dharma Seed audio rules (2026-10-20)

Dharma Seed (dharmaseed.org) hosts talks under terms that generalize to all
borrowed audio. The pattern below is written for Dharma Seed and applies to
any CC BY-NC-ND or gift-of-dhamma audio.

## The licence (what it demands)

- **Complete and unchanged**: play talks whole. No excerpts, no remixes,
  no background-music beds, no speed beyond the player's own control.
- **Credited**: teacher name + talk title + source (Dharma Seed) on every
  play surface and in every playlist/curation referencing it.
- **Non-commercial**: no paywall, no ads against playback, no premium tier
  gating talks. The app around them can have paid features; the talks cannot.
- **Metadata only in our records**: title/speaker/duration/links. Never
  transcripts (05 doctrine holds for talks too).

## What the engine may and may not do

- MAY: slot full talks into timetables, build playlists, recommend by
  topic/tradition, show progress through a talk.
- MAY NOT: cut passages for retreats (virtual clips exist for discovery,
  not for practice playback), auto-play talks back-to-back as background,
  transcribe, translate, or summarize content as a substitute for listening.
- Teacher interviews and Q&A recordings stay clearly labelled as recordings
  of someone else's interview — never presented as answers to the listener.

## Per-publisher check before wiring a new source

Licence page read + recorded in licence-registry (28) with the exact grant
quoted. No licence found = link-only, no playback integration.
