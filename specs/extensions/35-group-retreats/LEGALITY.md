# LEGALITY — structuring retreats from Dharma audio (2026-10-20)

*Not legal advice. The pattern below is conservative by design: it stays
inside what licences and fair use clearly allow, and flags the rest for
teachers and the owner.*

## The Burbea-jhana-retreat question: yes, if five rules hold

1. **Stream, don't rehost.** Audio plays from Dharma Seed (embed/link),
   files live on their servers, their bandwidth. Never copy MP3s into our
   bundles, caches or training data.
2. **Complete, unchanged, credited, non-commercial** (their terms + 33's
   DHARMA-SEED-RULES). Full talks in the timetable slots; teacher + title +
   source on every surface; no paywall or ads against playback.
3. **Our layer is ours.** Schedules, rules, timers, playlists-as-orderings,
   reflections and progress tracking are original functional expression —
   fine. The retreat's VALUE (sequencing + discipline + tracking) is ours;
   the teachings stay theirs.
4. **No endorsement implied.** "A retreat structured around Rob Burbea's
   jhana teachings (via Dharma Seed)" — nominative, attributed, with a
   link to Gaia House. Never "Burbea's retreat" or his photo as branding.
   Living teachers get asked first as courtesy even when the licence
   doesn't require it; deceased teachers' organisations get the same note.
5. **Proof of retreat is self-attestation.** "Kept the schedule" logs are
   the user's own data (profile store, 10). They prove discipline to the
   user and their teacher if shared — never certificates of attainment,
   never lineage credentials.

## Group/teacher-hosted retreats: the extra rules

- Teachers opt in and claim their rooms. The platform never lists a living
  teacher's retreat without them (or their org) saying yes in writing.
- Recordings policy belongs to the teacher (default: no recording, per
  GATHER-SPEC trust rules; opt-in only, announced up front).
- Dana flows to teachers/orgs directly. The platform takes no cut, charges
  no fee on dharma, and never holds donations. Paid courses (Tergar-style
  cohorts) are the ORG's product on the ORG's terms — we list and link.
- Safeguarding before open rooms (minors policy, reporting path) — blocks
  launch, not a backlog item.
