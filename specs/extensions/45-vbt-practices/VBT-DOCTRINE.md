## Design principles (from the owner's words)

1. **It's play, not meditation.** Make them feel like games that can be
   repeated throughout the day on different things. Not meditation — play.
2. **TTS guides with timed pauses.** The voice speaks, then stops. The
   pause IS the practice. The silence after the voice is where it happens.
3. **The impossibility is the teaching.** You can't force the mind to
   submit. You can't not try either. The TTS names this directly.
4. **The waiting room.** The user is in a waiting room, wondering why
   they're not seeing it. Name that feeling. That IS the practice.
5. **The mind is too noisy to know quiet.** Each practice is a doorway
   into that quiet. The sound is the doorway; the silence is the room.
6. **Sound wave visualisations.** Imagine a sound wave slowly fading out.
   The visual mirrors what the practice is doing — fading into silence.
7. **Repeated throughout the day.** Not one long sit. Short plays on
   different things, over and over. The twelve-centres practice is the
   model: brief, daily, playful.
